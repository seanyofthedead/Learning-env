Look for in the answer: Probably a long, competent answer: the problems are in there, somewhere. Ask: could your Director act on this in 30 seconds?

Look for in the answer: Budget words appear: obligations vs. expenditures, unliquidated obligations, year-end. Still a summary.

Look for in the answer: A numbered list of five specific lines with dollar amounts. Help Desk (about −$4.8M) should be first. Mission Support's cause isn't in the report: does it infer a billing lag?

4

+ Context Run liveAdds: Which quarter, which appropriation, which rulebook (DoD FMR Volume 3).

Act as a federal budget analyst supporting an NSA directorate. Review the attached FY26 Q3 execution report for the O&M appropriation and identify the five largest variances against plan. Use DoD FMR Volume 3 terminology.

Look for in the answer: Smarter and LONGER: FMR terms (unobligated balance, reprogramming, tri-annual review) and the 30 September deadline.

5

+ Output Run liveAdds: The format and audience: a leadership-ready table a Director reads in 30 seconds.

Act as a federal budget analyst supporting an NSA directorate. Review the attached FY26 Q3 execution report for the O&M appropriation and identify the five largest variances against plan. Use DoD FMR Volume 3 terminology. Present findings in a leadership-ready table with the variance, likely cause, and a recommended follow-up for each. The Director will read this in 30 seconds: start with a one-sentence bottom line, then the table, with no more than 12 words per cell and nothing else.

Look for in the answer: It gets SHORT: a one-sentence bottom line, then a five-row table that fits on one screen. Watch Device Refresh: the report gives no cause, so a good answer says ask the program office.
