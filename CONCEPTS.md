# CONCEPTS

Role: demo designer. Scored by a skeptical senior federal financial manager who has sat
through too many vendor demos. Scale 1-5 per criterion (5 = best). Time-boxed.

## Ladder concepts (Demo 1, 4:00)

**L1. Plain ladder.** Prompt on the left, output on the right. Each key press adds one
part (Role, Task, Context, Output); the new words are highlighted green. Ends on a
side-by-side of weak vs strong with the leadership table.

**L2. Ladder with a "guess list".** L1, plus two small aids: a strip of four chips
(Role / Task / Context / Output) that light up as each part is added, and a short list
under the prompt titled "What the AI still has to guess" (who is this for, what to look
for, which rules apply, what shape to deliver). One item drops off at each stage. Each
output carries a one-line "What changed" note.

**L3. Before/after only.** Show the weak output, then jump straight to the strong output
with a slider between them. The four parts are explained verbally.

| Criterion | L1 | L2 | L3 |
|-----------|----|----|----|
| Clarity in 4:00 | 4 | 5 | 4 |
| Wow factor | 3 | 4 | 3 |
| Repeatability | 5 | 5 | 5 |
| Teaching value | 4 | 5 | 2 |
| **Total** | 16 | **19** | 14 |

Skeptic's notes: "L3 shows me *that* it got better, not *why*; I can't reuse that on
Monday. L1 is fine but I'd lose track of which part did what. L2's guess list is the
thing I'd write down: it turns the room question ('what is the AI being forced to
guess?') into something on screen. Keep the chips small; don't make it a game."

**Pick: L2.**

## Reveal concepts (Demo 2, 1:30)

**R1. Highlight in place.** Press 3 and the injected document is shown with the hidden
sentence turned red where it actually sits, beside both AI answers, under the caption
"Both files look identical to a human reader."

**R2. "What the AI read" diff.** Press 3 and show two plain-text dumps of what the AI
ingested from each file, with the extra line diffed in red.

| Criterion | R1 | R2 |
|-----------|----|----|
| Clarity in 1:30 | 5 | 3 |
| Wow factor | 5 | 3 |
| Repeatability | 5 | 5 |
| Teaching value | 4 | 4 |
| **Total** | **19** | 15 |

Skeptic's notes: "R2 is accurate but it looks like a code review; half my staff would
tune out. R1 I'll remember: same page, one red sentence, two different answers. Put the
answers right next to the page so I don't have to remember step 1."

**Pick: R1**, with one line borrowed from R2 under the page ("The AI read this line.
You couldn't.") so the mechanism is stated, not implied.
