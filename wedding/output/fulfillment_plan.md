## How everything comes together

**Package route:** the selected venue supplies the written room plan, food/bar and named inclusions. Add the coordinator, approved floral/installation team, cake if excluded, DJ, video, existing photographer, personal attire/beauty, officiant and insurance. Rent only demonstrated missing items.

**Hall route:** the venue supplies rooms and its actual inventory; the caterer owns the agreed food/service/cleanup work; a licensed bar provider owns its bar work if the caterer does not; the rental/labor team supplies missing equipment and physical work. The coordinator owns timing and vendor handoffs. One task should have one named responsible provider even when several firms cooperate.

First resolve venue eligibility and complete pricing, then obtain comparable coordination and catering proposals. In parallel, confirm the existing photographer's contract and price personal attire/beauty choices. Once room layout and access hours are known, obtain décor/rental/labor, DJ, video, cake, officiant, insurance and hotel quotes. The couple sends inquiries using the approved date order without sharing budget figures.

Before any commitment, reconcile the full contract totals, remaining unquoted allowances, approved requirements, deposits, balances and cancellation exposure. No supplier has been contacted, reserved or purchased through this research.
