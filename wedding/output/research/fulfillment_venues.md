| Task | Responsible supplier | Evidence needed before paying |
|---|---|---|
| Heated ceremony, holding/cocktail and reception rooms | Selected venue event manager, coordinated with caterer | One dimensioned layout and room timeline showing all 185, dancing retained, access, accessibility and a priced flip where needed |

| Parking | Venue or its approved parking operator | Car capacity, accessible dropoff, guest/vendor fees or waiver, nighttime pickup and removal rules |
| Security | Venue-approved licensed contractor or required police detail | Whether required; staff count, hours and charge; confirm whether included before hiring separately |
| Alcohol license/permit | Venue license holder or approved licensed caterer, with the relevant government issuer if needed | Who buys and serves alcohol, license coverage, required permit, insurance, fee and deadline |
| Wedding-night room | Selected hotel | Actual date-specific room invoice within the $600 allowance, tax/parking, cancellation and payment terms |

Security, parking and alcohol permissions depend on the selected venue's rules. Selecting a random standalone security or valet firm now could duplicate an included service or fail the venue's approval process. Assign the named provider through the venue's written proposal. A dry-hire bartender alone does not establish alcohol-license coverage.
