## 2. MICKNICS: compare one coordinated tabletop proposal

[MICKNICS tabletop styling](https://www.micknics.com/event-tablescape-venue-styling) publishes a 15-plus-table tier of **$75 per table**, covering tables with up to eight seats, plus a styling/curation fee starting at **$175**. Its listed components include chargers, napkins, runners, candles, a small faux centerpiece or four bud vases, selected welcome/place-card elements, and delivery/setup/breakdown.

At **24 eight-seat tables**, the headline calculation is **$1,975 before tax and additional charges**. The current 19-table plan assumes ten seats, which requires a custom price. This bundle could combine work and products now spread across several lines, but it is not an established saving. Request itemization so centerpiece, candle, specialty-linen/charger and labor costs are charged once. Do not assume runners replace full tablecloths, or welcome/place cards replace a seating chart and table numbers.

The supplier separately advertises real dinnerware **from $30 per person**. That is not included in the tabletop headline and could duplicate venue/caterer china. Fresh personal flowers can remain with a florist; faux reception arrangements are an appearance option to consider, not an assumed replacement for the approved fresh bouquet.

[Plain arch rentals](https://www.micknics.com/wedding-arch-rentals-maryland) start at **$75**, and graduated sets at **$125**, with up-to-12-hour delivery/setup/breakdown wording. Confirm the exact location, travel, final pickup time and applicable fees; the site's differently scoped styling/rental descriptions do not establish one universal delivered price. [Rental inventory](https://www.micknics.com/wedding-event-party-rentals-maryland) lists real-flame candle options, but enclosure dimensions and venue approval still need confirmation.

An alternative is [A&A's plain white lattice arch](https://anarentalstation.com/bridal-equipment/) at **$50 for one day**, plus unquoted delivery and installation. Its $13 easel can support a separately printed seating chart. These are simple rental products, not flower-covered installations.

## 4. Physical labor: name the crew before calling the design fulfilled

**First standalone labor inquiry: [Elilta Events](https://www.eliltaevents.com/faq).** Its official FAQ describes décor setup, table settings, breakdown, cleanup and setup/breakdown crews across DC, Maryland and Northern Virginia. Rates and minimum hours are unpublished. Request the crew size, paid hours, split-shift/return-trip costs, venue credentials, insurance, late departure and each physical task. No evidence yet establishes that all required work fits $700.

**Use the rental suppliers for their own equipment where practical.** A&A's setup rate covers setting out its furniture, not flower placement, dishwashing or a mid-event flip. MICKNICS describes installation/removal of its rented décor; handling a separate florist's products or client-owned items needs explicit agreement. Its full-venue design fee starts at $1,500 before rentals/materials, so that broader service is not a substitute for a $700 labor quote.

Assign unloading, table/chair placement, table settings, flower/candle placement, ceremony moves, flip, bussing/washing, repacking, trash and final collection to one responsible provider each. Coordination is supervision and timing unless the contract explicitly adds workers.

For [The Villa](https://www.thevillaweddingvenue.com/faq/), added flowers and décor require licensed-professional placement **and removal**, with exceptions to be discussed. An à la carte order therefore needs a venue-approved professional crew or written exception. Venue-specific rules control every design; enclosed candles remain required, and LEDs are not a default workaround.
