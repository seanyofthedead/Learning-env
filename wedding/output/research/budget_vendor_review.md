## 5. Tips: prevent duplicate spending without assuming free labor

Retain the **$2,000** trimmed allowance. It is $2,000 once, not a percentage added again to the modeled service charge. No researched source establishes the actual crew count or a mandatory wedding-wide tipping amount for this event.

Create a small internal ledger when proposals arrive: vendor, required service/admin charge, required gratuity, who receives it, discretionary amount, paid status. Ask whether each required charge reaches the staff. A charge labelled “service” should not be assumed to be a tip; equally, a mandatory gratuity already included in the venue/caterer model should not be paid a second time from the tips allowance. Explicitly label any additional discretionary tip. This is a budgeting procedure, not a universal tipping rule.

Existing official evidence shows why the distinction matters: Famous Dave's wedding menu excludes tax/gratuity; MISSION BBQ lists staffing with gratuity additional; Picoso calls its 15% charge “gratuity/service.” These descriptions are not interchangeable.
