## Written answers needed before calling it a fit

Ask for a wedding packet, dimensioned floor plans, a complete proposal and the wedding contract. The decisive answers are:

- All 185 at rounds with a usable dance floor throughout the reception.
- A fully heated indoor ceremony/holding plan and separate cocktail area, with every flip charge and staffing obligation stated.
- Availability on an acceptable date, sufficient access for setup/cleanup, and permitted reception hours.
- Written outside DJ/florist/cake and enclosed-candle permissions.
- An itemized 2027 total: rental, minimum spend, food, bar hours, bartenders, service charge, tax, gratuity treatment, ceremony/flip, rooms, equipment, coordinator, parking, security, cleaning and overtime. Reconcile vendor meals against the separate existing $400 allowance once, without double counting.
- Deposit, payment schedule, hold expiry, booking window, cancellation/rescheduling, insurance and price-escalation terms. Golf-outing deposits and tee-time cancellation rules do not establish wedding terms.

**Decision rule:** mark “fits” only when every required feature is established and all venue-driven costs total no more than $31,139. A total from $31,139.01 to $32,139 is “edge”; a higher price or a failed must-have is “no.” Missing price or layout evidence remains “unknown.”
