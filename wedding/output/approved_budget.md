## Required checks and spending priorities

Protect food and service, comfortable heated ceremony arrangements, separate cocktail space, clear sound, dancing and adequate coordination. Compare a complete venue package with a complete hall/catering bill. Keep outside DJ/florist/cake permissions, enclosed candles and a reception ending at least 10pm, preferably 11pm. A priced room flip must have a suitable holding room and assigned workers.

A venue's financial result is **fits** at $31,139 or less, **edge** above that through $32,139, and **no** above the hard ceiling. Overall fit also requires complete pricing and verified requirements; an under-cap estimate with missing evidence is **unknown**. No reserve-spending alternative is part of the approved plan. The model now distinguishes these results and charges cake cutting separately when needed even if cake is included.

Keep the photographer's booked $750. Retain the coordinator, sound, video, insurance and parking allowances until a written quote proves the same required service is included elsewhere. A service/admin fee is not automatically a staff gratuity. Reconcile required gratuity and intended tips against the existing $2,000 once.
