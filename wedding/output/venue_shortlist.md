## How to read and reproduce the prices

- The placeholder is food $60 + staff $15 + beer/wine $20 + soft drinks $3, then 20% service, tax and three $200 bartenders. The model gives **$128.81/guest in MD**, not the prompt's rough $124. No caterer has offered this full package.
- Known older venue/package prices rise 4% per year to 2027. An undated page stays “year unknown”; a 2026 baseline where needed is explicitly a planning assumption. PDF metadata is a historical sensitivity, not proof of the period when a rate applied.
- Unpublished-package specs use **calculated quote ceilings**, labeled SCENARIO in the input. Their totals near $32,139 are deliberately solved limits, not discovered prices or evidence that the venue fits. All seven stay unknown.
- Missing facilities use the prescribed rental, cake, getting-ready and parking allowances. Unquoted mandatory charges are disclosed as gaps rather than silently asserted to be zero. Refundable security deposits affect cash timing and must be tracked separately from costs.
- Confirmed fixes add Prince William 9%, Vienna 10% and Fairfax City 10.5%, and repair the model's rental-tax solver. Maryland alcohol is 9%; the 12% statute concerns cannabis. Tax depends on invoice details and, for some Virginia catering, the origin of sale. See [official tax checks](research/tax_checks.md) for DC's scheduled 7% rental-tax evidence and limitations, including conservative Maryland service-charge taxation.
- The inherited model labels outside vendors/end time/candles as “nice to check.” **This shortlist treats them as requirements.** Its alternative reserve-spending verdict is not used: the reserve remains untouched. Fits would require at least $1,000 headroom and all requirements supported; edge would mean less headroom or a qualifying low scenario. None is confirmed in either group today.
- Previously rejected leads remain rejected and were not revived. No prohibited marketplace pages were accessed. If you originally found any venue on **Peerspace**, direct booking can trigger its **30% off-platform fee**; check that obligation before proceeding.
